What is the analemma?
If you photograph the sun at the same clock time every day for a year and overlay the images, you get a figure-8. Two effects cause this:
Vertical axis — Earth's axial tilt (23.45°). Moves the sun north/south through the year.
Horizontal axis — Equation of Time. Earth's elliptical orbit causes the sun to be up to ±16min early or late vs a clock.
Latitude tilt: At the equator the analemma lies nearly horizontal. As latitude increases toward the poles, it tilts upright.

12h vs 24h — Why it matters
Every ambiguity in 12hr simply doesn't exist in 24hr. This is arithmetic, not opinion.
The core problem: "Meet at 6" — morning or evening? In 24hr: 06:00 and 18:00 are never confused. Not once. Anywhere on earth.
Two clocks, same time — one ambiguous, one not
12-hour clock
—
AM/PM carries all the meaning. Remove it — the time is meaningless.
Is this 07:30 or 19:30? The hands cannot answer.
vs
24-hour clock
—
Hour hand takes 24 hours for one rotation. At any position exactly one time is possible.
Exactly one possible reading. Always.
